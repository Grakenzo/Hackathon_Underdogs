ICT Officer - Application Support
Indie Education
Brisbane QLD
Help Desk & IT Support (Information & Communication Technology)
Full time
$80,364 + Super + Salary Packaging

Indie Education is an educational hub and home to a family of training and educational programs, providing learning services for all age groups.

Job Description:
In this role, you will provide expert support, administration, and enhancement of business applications, ensuring reliable operation, successful deployment, timely troubleshooting and positive user experiences for employees across multiple sites and business units.

Preferred skills and experience:
- Proven experience providing application support in a multi-site environment.
- Strong capability in application deployment, lifecycle management, patching, upgrades, and system configuration.
- Advanced troubleshooting skills, including the ability to interpret logs, analyse error patterns, isolate variables, and identify root causes.
- Demonstrated expertise in Microsoft 365 administration, including SharePoint, Teams, OneDrive and Exchange, together with advanced skills in Microsoft Power Platform development and governance, Microsoft Intune application and device management, and Entra ID security group administration.
- Familiarity with cloud-based platforms and the ability to learn new applications quickly.
- Experience working with ticketing systems and maintaining accurate support records.
- Strong communication skills, with the ability to explain technical concepts clearly to non-technical users.
- Experience creating high-quality documentation, training materials, and process guides.

Essential Requirements:
For this role, applicants must be willing and able to obtain:
- Current Driver's licence.
- National Police Check (issued within 3 months prior to appointment) and renewed annually.
- Relevant State Working with Children's Check.
- Unrestricted working rights in Australia.
