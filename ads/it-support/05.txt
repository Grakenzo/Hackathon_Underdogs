About NetVault:
NetVault connects Australian businesses to the internet, cloud, and each other — through a network spanning ten Tier 3/4 data centres across Brisbane, Sydney, Melbourne, and Perth.

The Role:
We're looking for a technically curious Level 1/2 Service Desk Engineer to join our close-knit Brisbane CBD team.

What You'll Be Doing:
- Handling Level 1 and Level 2 inbound support via phone and email
- Triaging issues, owning the resolution process, and keeping customers informed
- Escalating and coordinating with Level 3 engineers when required
- Assisting with NOC functions, including communicating scheduled and unscheduled outages
- Ordering and coordinating NBN and business internet services with wholesale carriers
- Assisting channel partners with VoIP, SIP, and telephony requirements
- Configuring Hosted PBX and 3CX systems
- Preparing, configuring, and deploying routers, firewalls, and CPE at customer premises
- Travelling to customer sites for on-site installations and fault resolution
- Contributing to internal documentation

Essential:
- Excellent verbal and written English — this is a customer-facing role
- A genuine passion for technology and problem-solving
- Helpdesk process knowledge and an understanding of escalation paths
- Foundational networking knowledge: TCP/IP, VLANs, routing & switching basics, firewalls
- Some exposure to NBN technologies
- Level 1 applicants: any hands-on IT support experience (work, study, or personal projects counts)
- Level 2 applicants: 2+ years of technical support experience in an IT, ISP, or telco environment
- Current Australian driver's licence

Highly Regarded:
- Degree or diploma in IT, Network Engineering, or a related discipline
- Previous experience at an ISP, MSP, or telecommunications provider
- VoIP / SIP telephony hands-on experience
- Cisco switching and routing (CCNA or equivalent knowledge)
- Virtualisation skills — VMware, Hyper-V, KVM
- Microsoft Server basics: Active Directory, DNS, DHCP, Exchange
- Certifications
