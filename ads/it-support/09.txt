Junior Systems Administrator
DataMC
Brisbane QLD
Networks & Systems Administration (Information & Communication Technology)
Full time
Salary undisclosed

About the role:
You will join DataMC's Infrastructure and IT Operations team as a Junior Systems Administrator. This is an entry-level development opportunity where you will receive practical training while learning DataMC's systems, processes and technology environment from the ground up.

Key responsibilities:
- Support Microsoft 365, Microsoft Entra ID, Intune and Azure
- Prepare and manage Windows and Linux devices
- Create and maintain user accounts, permissions and security groups
- Support employee onboarding and offboarding across multiple countries
- Troubleshoot device, software, access and connectivity issues
- Assist with system monitoring, backups, updates and patching
- Investigate networking and cloud-service issues
- Write and maintain clear technical documentation
- Use PowerShell, Bash and Python for administration and automation
- Follow security policies, change-control requirements and approved workflows

About you:
- Curious and eager to learn
- Logical and methodical when investigating problems
- Comfortable asking questions when unsure
- Security-conscious and careful when making changes
- Organised, reliable and attentive to detail
- Able to communicate clearly with technical and non-technical people
- Patient and respectful when supporting employees and clients

Helpful but not essential:
- Current or completed study in IT, computer science, cybersecurity, data or a related field
- Basic knowledge of Windows or Linux
- Familiarity with Linux command-line tools
- Understanding of networking concepts such as TCP/IP, DNS, Wi-Fi and VPNs
- Awareness of cloud-computing concepts
- Familiarity with Microsoft 365
- Basic knowledge of PowerShell, Bash, Python or another scripting language
- Basic knowledge of SQL or relational databases
- Entry-level certifications such as AZ-900, SC-900 or MS-900
