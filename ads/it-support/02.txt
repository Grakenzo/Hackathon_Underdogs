About us:
Clubfit Software is a leading provider of Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) solutions designed specifically for the fitness and wellness industry.

About the role:
We are seeking a Technical Support/Admin IT Helpdesk professional to join Clubfit Software in Brisbane, QLD on a full-time basis. You will provide comprehensive technical support to our SaaS gym software customers whilst also managing IT administration tasks internally. You will be the first line of contact for customers experiencing technical difficulties.

Key responsibilities:
- Provide first-line technical support to gym software customers via phone, email, chat and ticketing systems
- Diagnose, troubleshoot and resolve technical issues related to the Clubfit SaaS platform, including account access, features, integrations and data synchronisation
- Document all customer interactions and resolutions in the support management system
- Escalate complex technical issues to the development and engineering teams when necessary
- Create and maintain technical documentation, knowledge base articles and FAQs
- Assist with user onboarding and training
- Manage internal IT administration tasks, including user account provisioning, software licensing, hardware troubleshooting and network support
- Identify trends in customer issues and provide feedback to improve product quality

What we're looking for:
- Proven experience in technical support or helpdesk roles, ideally supporting SaaS applications
- Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills
- Proficiency with support ticketing systems and CRM platforms
- Solid understanding of IT infrastructure, including networks, servers, cloud-based applications
- Experienced in SQL
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Customer-focused mindset
- Ability to work independently and manage multiple support requests
- Familiarity with fitness industry software is desirable but not essential
- Relevant IT certifications (such as CompTIA A+, Microsoft Certified Associate) are advantageous
