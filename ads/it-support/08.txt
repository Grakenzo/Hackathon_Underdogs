About Fitness & Lifestyle Group:
FLG is Asia Pacific's largest group of corporately owned health and wellness clubs — 310+ locations, 600,000+ members, and five of the region's most recognised fitness brands.

The opportunity:
This role is based in our modern, open-plan support office in the vibrant heart of South Brisbane. You will play a crucial role in ensuring the seamless operation of our information technology network, applications, and desktop equipment.

Key duties and responsibilities:
- Manage and take ownership of IT request tickets through the entire lifecycle
- Provide exceptional customer experience via phone, chat and self-service to trouble-shoot solutions for hardware and operating systems issues
- Act as an escalation point for Level 1 and 2 support
- Run Major Incident Protocols as required
- Provide hands on support to Staff at our West End support office
- Provide after-hours support as part of an on-call roster

Essential skills / experience:
- A few years experience in a similar system administration or ICT Support role / Tertiary qualifications in Information Technology (or equivalent professional experience)
- Strong knowledge and experience of Microsoft Stack applications and tools including Microsoft 365, Exchange, Azure and Endpoint Manager
- Strong diagnostic, problem-solving, and documentation skills
- Proactive with the ability to work autonomously
- Strong customer centric approach with excellent oral and written communication skills
- Managed Service Provider (MSP) experience

Desirable experience:
- Certificate III in IT or similar qualification
- MS-900 or CompTIA++ equivalent
- Atlassian Admin Experience (Jira, Confluence)
- Retail/Leisure industry knowledge and experience
