Help Desk & IT Support (Information & Communication Technology)

We're looking for an experienced technical team member who can investigate and resolve issues independently, take ownership through to resolution and provide a high level of technical support across the organisation.

You'll work across Microsoft 365, Google suite, Windows, Apple and Linux endpoints and our wider network infrastructure.

The role:
- Provide all levels of technical support across the organisation.
- Support Microsoft 365, Google Suite and endpoint environments.
- Manage user accounts, permissions, access and shared mailboxes.
- Troubleshoot hardware, software, application and connectivity issues.
- Investigate and resolve more complex technical escalations.
- Support our networking and infrastructure environments.
- Assist with cyber security remediation and improvement initiatives.
- Contribute to infrastructure and technology projects.
- Coordinate with technology vendors and specialist support teams.

About you — you'll ideally have:
- Strong Level of IT support experience with the capability to troubleshoot more complex issues.
- Hands-on Microsoft 365 administration, configuration and troubleshooting experience.
- Experience with Google suite to Microsoft 365 migration.
- Experience provisioning and supporting laptops, mobile devices and endpoints.
- Experience with Intune or similar endpoint management tools.
- Strong troubleshooting skills across hardware, software and connectivity.
- Some exposure to networking, infrastructure, cyber security or technology projects.
- An understanding of Cloud technologies including Software as a Service.
- The ability to investigate and resolve issues independently while knowing when to escalate.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills at all levels.

This role is an internal On-Site IT role with limited customer interaction.
