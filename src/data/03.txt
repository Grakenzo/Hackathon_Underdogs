IT Support Technician (Level 1) — Part-time
Defy IT | Indooroopilly, Brisbane QLD | Part-time, 3 days / 22.5 hours per week
$27-$30 per hour + super + km allowance

About Us:
Defy IT is a Brisbane-based managed service provider looking after around 40 small businesses across legal, healthcare, accounting, professional services and trades.

The Role:
We're after a Level 1 technician to take first-line support across our client base, with a growth path to Level 2 as you build depth. The split is roughly 75% technical, 25% business support.

What You'll Do:
- First-line support via phone, email, remote tools and onsite visits
- Troubleshoot and resolve hardware, software and network issues
- Install and configure workstations, printers, and network equipment
- Support Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Windows 10/11 and Windows Server environments
- Onsite visits to small-business clients across greater Brisbane
- Document work properly in our PSA and documentation platforms
- Assist with scheduling, quoting and hardware ordering
- Escalate cleanly — knowing when to hand something up is a skill we value

Our Stack:
NinjaOne RMM, SuperOps, Microsoft 365 and Entra ID, Windows Server, UniFi networking, and Proxmox. Familiarity is a bonus, not a requirement.

What You'll Bring:
- Certificate IV or Diploma in Information Technology, or equivalent hands-on experience
- Around 12 months in an IT support role with fieldwork experience
- Current driver's licence and a reliable vehicle — onsite work is part of the job
- Working knowledge of Windows, Microsoft 365, and networking fundamentals (DHCP, DNS, VLANs, VPN)
- Clear written and verbal communication
- Unrestricted working rights in Australia

Bonus points for:
- Active Directory, Group Policy, or basic Windows Server administration
- Azure, Google Workspace, or Microsoft 365 admin experience
- Networking beyond the basics — firewalls, VLANs, site-to-site VPN
- Certifications (CompTIA A+ or Network+, Microsoft Fundamentals, or similar)
- Linux Server Administration Experience

How to Apply:
Apply with your resume and a short note covering two things: the most interesting technical problem you've solved, and what you want to be doing in three years.

Employer questions:
- Which of the following statements best describes your right to work in Australia?
- How many years' experience do you have as a Computer Technician?
- Do you have a current Australian driver's licence?
- Do you have customer service experience?
- Do you have professional experience troubleshooting and repairing hardware issues on PC and laptop?
- Do you own or have regular access to a car?
