About Us:
Pioneer brand seeds are produced and distributed in Australia by GenTech Seeds, a Yates family business continuing a legacy of excellence in breeding and commercialising seed for Australian farmers for 50 years.

About the Role:
As an IT Support Specialist, you'll be the friendly face of tech support, ensuring our people and devices run smoothly and securely. You'll provide hands-on and remote support, troubleshoot issues, and help users get the most from their technology. You'll work with the latest Microsoft 365 tools, Windows 11, Teams, OneDrive, and more. This position is expected to be in our Brisbane office 5 days per week.

What You'll Do:
- Respond to support requests promptly, troubleshooting user and device issues in a Microsoft 365 environment.
- Log and categorise all support requests in our helpdesk system.
- Source, deploy, and maintain company devices (laptops, phones, tablets, peripherals).
- Manage user accounts—add, remove, and update users in core systems, mainly Entra ID
- Coach users on tools like Windows 11, Office365, Teams, OneDrive, and Outlook.
- Ensure all settings meet company security standards.
- Complete projects as directed (e.g., device refreshes).
- Deliver friendly, attentive support that boosts user satisfaction.

About You:
- Tertiary Diploma or equivalent in ICT (or working towards one).
- Industry certifications (e.g., CompTIA) are a plus.
- 2 years' experience in IT support or customer service.
- Basic understanding of computer hardware, Windows OS, networking, software, device management, and security best practices.
- Patient, well-organised, and a great communicator—especially when explaining tech to non-tech users.
- Process-oriented, with good judgement and a collaborative spirit.
- Eager to learn and develop your skills.
